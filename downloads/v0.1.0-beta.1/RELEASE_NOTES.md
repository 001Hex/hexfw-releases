## HexFW v0.1.0-beta.1: the first beta

**⚡ Install in one click:** [hex-modifications.github.io/flipper](https://hex-modifications.github.io/flipper/#download) (Chrome or Edge on a computer, Flipper plugged in by USB).

### What's new
- **Vaporwave desktop.** Two original idle scenes replace the stock animations: *Sunset Drive* (palm, striped sun and shimmering sea) and *Grid Run* (the HEX mark over a scrolling neon grid).
- Reports itself as **HexMods** to qFlipper, Flipper Lab and the Flipper mobile app.
- Everything else is the official Flipper Zero firmware **1.5.1** (app API 88.2), so catalogue apps keep working.

### Files
| File | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `flipper-z-f7-update-v0.1.0-beta.1.tgz` | qFlipper **Install from file**, Flipper Lab, or extract to `/update` on the SD card |
| `flipper-z-f7-full-v0.1.0-beta.1.dfu` | Advanced: DFU flashing |
| `SHA256SUMS` | Check your download (`sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`) |

> **Beta:** not yet tested on physical hardware by Hex Modifications. Recovery and going back to official firmware: see the [README](https://github.com/001Hex/hexfw-releases#recovery).
